ABOUT THE DEMO

Welcome to Namma Space

This is a front-end prototype with a sample building, searchable points of interest, and grid-based A* pathfinding. The map is illustrative, not a real venue map.

  • Choose a destination to draw a route.
  • Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
  • Pathfinding avoids the sample walls and blocked cells.

To use this for a real building, replace the sample floor plan and coordinates with verified venue data.
